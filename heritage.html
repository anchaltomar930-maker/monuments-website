INDIAN HERITAGE

Stories carved in stone

Understanding India's architectural and cultural legacy

Why Are Monuments Important?

Historical monuments are an important part of India's cultural heritage. They preserve stories of the past and introduce us to the artistic and architectural styles of different periods.


Major Elements of Indian Heritage

  • Architecture: Unique designs, structures and construction techniques.

  • History: Monuments help us understand important events and rulers.

  • Culture: They reflect the traditions and lifestyles of different periods.

  • Art: Sculptures, paintings, carvings and decorative designs are found in many monuments.
